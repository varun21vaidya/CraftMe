About this piece

Painted over the monsoon of 2025 from the terrace of a Mylapore house, looking towards the Marina. The sky is built from eleven thin glazes of ultramarine and teal, then broken by a single dark sun. The canvas is stretched on a 3.5 cm deep frame, so it hangs without a frame.
