Questions about buying

Read the refund policy

Who posts my Order?

The artist who made it, within 7 days of payment.

Can I cancel an Order?

Not once it is Paid. Write to CraftMe and we will ask the artist.

Can I buy from two artists at once?

Each Order is one parcel from one artist, so a Cart holds one artist's work.

Do you ship outside India?

No. CraftMe ships within India only.

Are the Reviews real?

Only Buyers whose Order was Received can review an artist, and CraftMe approves every Review.
