Terms of use

1. What CraftMe is

CraftMe is a marketplace where independent Artists sell physical original artwork and prints to Buyers in India. Buyers pay CraftMe. The Artist ships the parcel. CraftMe pays the Artist afterwards.

2. Your Account

You need an Account to buy, sell or review. One person, one Account. Keep your password private. You can hold more than one role, for example Buyer and Artist, but an Artist can't buy their own Listings.

3. Buying

  • Prices are in Indian rupees and include shipping within India.
  • When you press Pay, the pieces are held for you for 15 minutes.
  • An Order is Paid once your payment succeeds. Paid Orders can't be cancelled by the Buyer.
  • Artists must ship within 7 days of payment.

4. Reviews

You can review an Artist once per Order, from the day it is Received for 30 days. Reviews appear after CraftMe approves them. Don't post personal details or anything unlawful.

5. Reporting

You can report an Artwork or Artist. CraftMe may take a Listing down or Restrict an Artist at its discretion, with a reason.

Privacy policy

What we collect

  • Account: name, email, photo, phone (optional).
  • Orders: items, amounts, shipping address, payment references. Card details go to Razorpay and never touch CraftMe.
  • Artists: legal name, PAN, payout UPI or bank details, verified phone, and if connected, an Instagram token.

Who sees what

  • An Artist sees your shipping address only after your Order is Paid, and only for that Order.
  • Artist verification and payout details are visible only to that Artist and the CraftMe Admin.
  • Instagram: CraftMe stores post links, not copies of posts.

Your rights

You can edit your profile any time and ask us to delete your data. See "Delete my data".

Refunds and Payouts

For Buyers

  • Damaged parcel: report it with photos within 3 days of the Order being Received. CraftMe decides. If we agree, you get your money back for the damaged items.
  • Not shipped in 7 days: the Order is Overdue and CraftMe refunds it.
  • Changed your mind: Paid Orders can't be cancelled by the Buyer. Contact CraftMe and we'll ask the Artist.
  • Timing: refunds go to your original payment method and take 5 to 7 working days.

For Artists

  • An Order becomes Payable 3 days after it is Received, if there's no open Damage Report.
  • CraftMe pays Artists by UPI or bank transfer on the 1st and 15th of each month.
  • Payout = price paid, minus Refunds, minus CraftMe's Commission, minus the payment gateway fee.
  • A Refund made after you were paid becomes an Amount Owed, taken from your next Payouts. If it stays unsettled for 60 days, your Listings are hidden (Restricted) until it's settled.

Artist agreement

  • Everything you sell is your own work.
  • You pay a one-time, non-refundable onboarding fee.
  • You ship within 7 days, pack it so it arrives undamaged, and keep the Buyer updated.
  • You give true identity, PAN and payout details, and keep them current.
  • You accept CraftMe's decisions on Damage Reports, Overdue Orders and Refunds.
  • If you connect Instagram, you allow CraftMe to read your posts and reels so you can pick which to show. You can disconnect at any time.

Delete my data

CraftMe Account

You can delete your Account from Profile. We delete your profile, saved address and notifications within 30 days. Orders and Reviews are kept without your name.

Instagram data

If you connected Instagram, go to Dashboard, Instagram and choose Disconnect. We delete your access token and the posts you picked straight away. You can also remove CraftMe in your Instagram settings under Apps and websites, and Instagram tells us.
