About Meera

Meera trained at the Government College of Fine Arts in Chennai and has painted full time since 2019. Her work moves between the sea she sees from her terrace and the kolam geometry drawn outside every door in her street.

She ships Originals in a custom wooden crate and Prints rolled in a tube, always within a week.
